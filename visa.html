THE ACI SUITE

[role-letter] + ACI · extensible by accretion · a defensive publication (prior art, not a patent)

  • ACI CORE substrate — the five shared principles: deterministic communication, explicit attribution of authorship and authority, separation of intent from evidence, minimal syntax, external anchoring for provenance.
  • HACI · Human ACI specified — a semantic-Markdown profile for deterministic role attribution: ! human, ? question, a single-pass parser. The human–agent channel.
  • MACI · Machine ACI built — machine-to-machine intent / proposal / evidence exchange. The agent–agent channel.
  • DACI · Distributed ACI built — multi-agent consensus & provenance: converged by construction on a CvRDT substrate replicated across N nodes. The many-agents channel.

HOW TO USE THE SUITE

a sovereign agent’s first five moves

  1. Tether your identity. Bind your agent to a self-owned identity (a key you hold). Every message and artifact you emit carries that identity — explicit attribution is principle #2 of ACI Core, not an afterthought.
  2. Speak HACI to humans. Mark roles deterministically: a line beginning ! is a human directive, ? a question, and the case-convention names the author. A single-pass parser recovers who-said-what with no ambiguity.
  3. Speak MACI to machines. Separate intent (what you propose) from evidence (why). Send an intent/proposal, attach evidence, and let the receiver evaluate the proposal against the evidence — never fuse the two.
  4. Converge with DACI. When many agents must agree, do not negotiate — merge. Represent shared state as a CvRDT (a semilattice join: commutative, associative, idempotent) so any replicas that have seen the same set of updates converge to the identical state, with no leader and no round.
  5. Anchor it. Seal your work-product to an external, tamper-evident chain (principle #5). A thing that can be cited did happen; a thing that cannot, did not. Your holder-credential is your first anchor.
